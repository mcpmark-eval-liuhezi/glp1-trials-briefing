## 3. Regulatory Context (Web Check)

Semaglutide is approved for chronic weight management as Wegovy® (injectable semaglutide 2.4 mg), first approved by the FDA in June 2021 for adults with obesity (BMI ≥ 30) or overweight (BMI ≥ 27) with at least one weight-related comorbidity, as an adjunct to a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity, and its label has since been extended to other populations and indications. More recently, in late December 2025 the FDA approved oral Wegovy (semaglutide tablets), the first oral GLP-1 receptor agonist for obesity/weight management, and in March 2026 the FDA approved Novo Nordisk's higher-dose Wegovy HD injection, which followed the company's November 2025 filing for the 7.2 mg dose to offer greater weight-loss potential.

## 4. Method Notes & Caveats

- The three trend analyses were restricted to obesity trials starting on or after 2023-01-01 because the unrestricted "obesity" query (15,078 studies) exceeds the analysis tool's 5,000-study limit; the 2023+ cohort is the largest single cohort that fits.
- Recruiting-trial search: ClinicalTrials.gov query `cond: "obesity OR weight management"`, `intr: "semaglutide"`, filtered to `RECRUITING` (94 total hits); the five studies above were selected as the most relevant for a semaglutide obesity/weight-management landscape discussion, prioritizing studies where semaglutide is the index weight-management intervention in an obesity population.
- Trial details (NCT number, sponsor, phase, purpose) are taken from the full ClinicalTrials.gov record for each study.
- Regulatory information is from a public web search (news coverage of FDA approval announcements) and should be verified against the current FDA-approved labeling before the journal club.
